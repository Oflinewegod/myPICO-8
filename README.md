## chess.p8

> you know chess, don't you?

This cartridge is the start of a working chess game. Currently, the white pawns are the only things that move, and I need to really think about how to check for move validity.

I was about to do a dictionary of every valid move that a piece could do (in terms of change in x/y from its original position), but then I didn't. I came back a different day and ended up doing it. I then realized why I didn't do it.

Imagine a rook. I would have to check every move from x + 1 to x + 7. I'm not doing that, the dictionary is gonna be huge. We'll see what I come up with.
